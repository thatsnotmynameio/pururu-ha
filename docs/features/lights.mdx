---
title: lights
description: Real lights inside a device, or relays that drive a lamp. Each one shows its light's state, offers what it offers and passes on what is asked of it.
---

`lights` puts real lights in a device: smart bulbs, or relays that turn a lamp on and off. Each one becomes a light of the device. It shows the real light's state, offers what the real light offers, such as brightness and colour, and turning it on, off or to another colour does the same to the real one.

```yaml
pururu:
  devices:
    biblioteca:
      name: Biblioteca
      area: biblioteca
      lights:
        teto: {entity: homeassistant.light.biblioteca_teto, name: Teto}
        abajur: {entity: homeassistant.switch.sonoff_abajur, name: Abajur}
```

This creates `light.pururu_biblioteca_light_teto`, shown as **Biblioteca Teto**, with what the bulb offers, and `light.pururu_biblioteca_light_abajur`, shown as **Biblioteca Abajur**, which turns on and off.

## Settings

`lights` is a map of **entity key → light**, with at least one light. The key is a slug, and it ends the entity ID, after the namespace `light`: `teto` → `light.pururu_biblioteca_light_teto`.

<Property name="entity" type="homeassistant.light.* or homeassistant.switch.*" required>
  The real light, written with `homeassistant.` before its entity ID, as everything Home Assistant owns: a `light.*` entity (`homeassistant.light.biblioteca_teto`), or a `switch.*` entity that drives a lamp, such as a relay (`homeassistant.switch.sonoff_abajur`). One entity, not a list. It can't be one of pururu's own lights or switches, even one you renamed in the UI. A renamed one is caught when pururu builds the device: that light isn't created, and the log says so.
</Property>

---

<Property name="name" type="string" required>
  The name shown after the device's name. It can't be empty. It's the same in every language: a light's name isn't translated.
</Property>

A light's key can be one another feature uses, such as `sprinkler` in both `lights` and `switches`: each feature has its own [namespace](/concepts/entity-ids#the-pattern), so `light.pururu_biblioteca_light_sprinkler` and `switch.pururu_biblioteca_switch_sprinkler` are two entities.

## Entity

| Entity | Path |
|---|---|
| `light.pururu_<key>_light_<entity key>` | `lights.<entity key>` |

The path is how the YAML names it: a [program](/concepts/programs#steps)'s step turns it on with `turn_on: lights.teto`, an alert or a reaction watches it with `when: lights.teto`, another device's reaction with `when: device.biblioteca.lights.teto`, and a group of [alert lights](/concepts/alert-lights) lists it as `device.biblioteca.lights.teto`.

| The real light or switch | `light.pururu_<key>_light_<entity key>` |
|---|---|
| `on` or `off` | the same |
| `unknown` | `unknown` |
| `unavailable`, or it doesn't exist | `unavailable` |

- **What it offers** is what the real entity offers, read every time it changes:
  - **A `light.*`** passes on its colour modes, brightness, colours, colour temperature and its range, effects, and whether it supports transitions and flashing. A bulb that only turns on and off gives a light that only turns on and off. If the bulb gains colour, after a firmware update for example, the pururu light follows without a reload.
  - **A `switch.*`** only turns on and off, so its light does too. Asking it for a brightness or a colour turns the relay on and ignores the rest.
- **Turning it on or off** does the same to the real entity, with the brightness, colour, effect or transition you asked for. The logbook names whoever did it: you, an automation or a voice assistant.
- If the real entity reports an **assumed state**, the pururu light does too.
- Its attribute **`entity_id`** names the real entity, and its details show it.
- It keeps no state of its own. After a restart, it shows the real entity again.

## A relay is a switch or a light

A relay that drives a lamp goes in `lights`, so voice assistants and area actions treat it as a light. In one device, a real entity can be in `lights` or in `switches`, not both:

```
switches: homeassistant.switch.sonoff_abajur is already in lights
```

Two devices can each use it their own way: a light in `biblioteca` and a switch in `varanda`.

## Alert lights

A light in a group of [alert lights](/concepts/alert-lights) shows alerts: while it does, its attributes **`alert`** (`high`, `medium`, `low` or `resolved`) and **`alerts`** say what for, and it keeps `alert` across restarts. Asked for an effect its bulb doesn't list, it passes on the rest without it.

## Good to know

- **Replacing the bulb or the relay.** Point `entity:` at the new one and reload. `light.pururu_biblioteca_light_teto` keeps its ID, name and area, so automations and dashboards keep working.
- **Two entities for one light.** The real light stays in its own device. If that device is in the same area, the area shows the light twice, and "turn off the lights in Biblioteca" reaches both. Turning both off is harmless, but a count of lights that are on counts two.
- **Renaming the real entity.** Update `entity:` in the YAML and reload. Until then, the pururu light is `unavailable`.
- **Renaming the pururu light** in the UI works as for any entity. See [Renaming an entity ID](/concepts/entity-ids#renaming-an-entity-id). Renamed to the entity its own `entity:` names, it stands for itself: it's kept, `unavailable`, and the log says so.
- **The same real light twice**, in one device or two, is fine: each pururu light follows it.
