---
title: Detected programs and phases
description: A program pururu tells from a reading, the appliance running at all, and the phases of each run, each a band of the plug's power.
---

A **detected program** is one pururu knows is running from a reading: a band of the plug's power, confirmed by delays. An appliance's `running_program` is one, the appliance running at all. Its **phases** are bands too: a washer warming, then wringing; a water purifier chilling or warming. The programs pururu runs itself are on [Programs](/concepts/programs).

```yaml
pururu:
  devices:
    clothes_washer:
      name: Tanquinho
      appliance:
        power: sensor.washer_plug_power
        running_program:
          above: 4
          on_delay: {minutes: 1}
          off_delay: {minutes: 2}
          statistics:
            runtime: [today, week, month, year]
            cycles: [today, week, month, year]
          phases:
            warming: {name: Aquecendo, above: 1000}
            wringing: {name: Centrifugando, above: 50, below: 1000, on_delay: {minutes: 3}}
```

## A band

<Property name="above, below" type="number" optional>
  The band is strictly **above** `above` and strictly **below** `below`, as Home Assistant's `numeric_state` trigger: with `above: 1000`, a reading of exactly 1000 is outside the band. At least one of them is required, and `above` must be lower than `below`.
</Property>

---

<Property name="on_delay" type="time period" optional>
  How long the reading must stay in the band before the run starts. Without it, 0: at once. A [time period](/features/appliance#settings).
</Property>

---

<Property name="off_delay" type="time period" optional>
  How long the reading must stay out of the band before the run ends. Without it, 0: at once.
</Property>

## The running program

`running_program` is required in an appliance's block: it's the appliance running at all. It has a band, then `statistics` (`runtime`, `cycles`: the appliance's own meters, see [Statistics](/concepts/statistics)), `phases` and `other`. It takes no `name`: it's named by the appliance, and a `name` there is a configuration error (`running_program takes no name: it is the appliance running`).

A cycle **starts** at the moment its `on_delay` passed, and **ends** at the moment the reading left the band; `off_delay` only confirms the end. A reading **without a value** (`unknown`, `unavailable`, not a number) counts every delay again from the next reading: what runs stays running, and an end already under way keeps the moment its reading left.

A **restart or reload** keeps the running cycle and its phases, from the detector's own snapshot, and Home Assistant's downtime isn't runtime. A restore that can't be read (a hand-edited `.storage`) starts from nothing.

## Phases

<Property name="name" type="string" required>
  How the phase's entities are named (`Aquecendo`, `Centrifugando`). It names the phase: without it, a phase is a configuration error.
</Property>

---

<Property name="above, below, on_delay, off_delay" type="—" optional>
  The same four as the program's own band, above.
</Property>

---

<Property name="statistics" type="map" optional>
  `runtime`, `cycles` and `energy` (which needs the appliance's `energy`). See [Statistics](/concepts/statistics).
</Property>

A phase's key is a slug. `idle` (the state while none runs) and `other` (the built-in phase, below) are **reserved**: `idle is a reserved phase key: name the phase otherwise`. A key whose entities would be another's is refused too, at the configuration:

- A phase keyed `current` or `last` collides with the detector's own: `phase current would create phase_current, the detector's`.
- One phase's totals can collide with another phase's key, such as `resfriar` beside a phase keyed `resfriar_cycles_total`: `phase resfriar_cycles_total would create phase_resfriar_cycles_total, phase resfriar's`.
- A phase keyed `resfriar_cycles_today` beside `resfriar`, whose **meter** (a period sensor, not a fixed entity key) it would be, is caught later, once every meter exists: `device <key>: <unique ID> would be two entities`.

### How phases run

- A phase runs while the program runs and its reading has held its band for its `on_delay`; it ends after its `off_delay` out of the band, dated when it left, or when the program ends.
- **Bands may overlap**: two phases that hold at once run at once, and none wins. `phase_current` is the one that started last.
- A **dip** straight from a running phase's band into another's makes the other wait, once its `on_delay` passed, for the first to end or for a reading back in both bands; it then starts from when its `on_delay` passed. A short visit to another band is nothing: a chill's tail dipping into a sip's band for a few seconds isn't a drink.
- An **idle gap**, a reading outside every band once a phase left its own (between `below: 40` and `above: 40`, or under the program's band), blocks nothing: the next phase starts after its own `on_delay`, and the first still ends, at the moment it left, unless its reading comes back.
- A phase whose band holds before the program runs starts with it; after the program ends, a band still holding starts with the next cycle; while the program's reading is out (its `off_delay` counting), no phase starts.
- At one instant: the program's end first, then the phases' ends in the order they started, then their starts in the configuration's order. A reading at the very instant a delay passes comes before it.

## `other`

`other` is the built-in phase: the program's band outside every configured phase's. It only exists with at least one phase configured (`other` without `phases`: `other is the program outside every phase: it needs phases`). It waits for nothing, and ends at once when a configured phase starts.

`other:` takes `on_delay` and `off_delay`, 30 seconds each by default (so a compressor's 2-second spike isn't a phase), and `statistics`. Write `other: {}`, or `other:` with its own settings, to ask for its meters.

## Entities

`<key>` is the device's key, `<phase>` a phase's key or `other`.

| Entity | What it is |
|---|---|
| `binary_sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_running` | The program: on while the appliance runs (see [`appliance`](/features/appliance#entities)) |
| `sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_phase_current` | The running phase that started last, or `idle`. Device class `enum`. Attributes: `running` (the running phases, in the configuration's order) and `seen` (the phases of the current or last cycle, `other` included, cleared when a cycle starts) |
| `sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_phase_last` | The phase of the last phase cycle that ended; `enum`, kept across restarts, `unknown` until the first |
| `binary_sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_phase_<phase>` | On while the phase runs, named by its `name` (`other`: "Other phase"). Attributes `cycle_start`, and `cycle_end` while its `off_delay` runs |
| `sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_phase_<phase>_last_cycle_start`, `_end`, `_duration`, `_energy` | The phase's last cycle, as the appliance's own (`_end` written last; `_energy` *with `energy`*) |
| `sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_phase_<phase>_cycles_total`, `_runtime_total`, `_energy_total` | Its totals, `total_increasing` (`_energy_total` added exactly, *with `energy`*) |
| `sensor.pururu_<key>_appliance_phase_<phase>_<counter>_<period>` | One per entry of the phase's (or `other`'s) `statistics` |

A phase's cycle entities are named after its `name` ("Aquecendo cycles", "Ciclos de Aquecendo"). `other`'s are named by their own translations ("Other phase cycles", "Ciclos de outra fase"). While `running` is disabled, every phase is off and `phase_current` is `idle`.

## State names

`phase_current` and `phase_last` show a phase's key. These names are translated in the UI (English and Portuguese):

| Name | English | Portuguese |
|---|---|---|
| `idle` | Idle | Parada |
| `rinsing` | Rinsing | Enxaguando |
| `drying` | Drying | Secando |
| `other` | Other | Outra |

Any other key, such as `warming` or `wringing`, is shown as written. `phase_last` never shows `idle`: it has no cycle to be one.

## Referring to a phase

An alert's or a reaction's `when` names a phase's entity key as it ends every entity ID: `appliance_phase_current`, `appliance_phase_resfriar`, `appliance_phase_resfriar_cycles_total`. A phase not configured, or any phase key of an appliance without phases, is refused at the configuration: `alerts: appliance_phase_morno is not an entity key of another feature of this device`.

Renaming a phase's entity in the UI is followed, as every pururu entity: `sensor.pururu_clothes_washer_appliance_phase_current follows binary_sensor.pururu_clothes_washer_appliance_running, which is not created; not creating it` if the program itself can't be created.
