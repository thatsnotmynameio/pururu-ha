---
title: Notifications
description: Simple news, told once to your phone. How it differs from an alert, and how a device tells it.
---

An **alert** is something you find critical: a problem that lasts, such as a door open that shouldn't be or a plug gone offline. It's a binary sensor, on while the problem lasts, and [Alert2](/concepts/alerts#getting-notified) tells you about it with reminders until it's resolved.

A **notification** is simple news, told **once**: the washer finished. It has no state, nothing to resolve, no reminder. pururu tells it through Home Assistant's `notify` actions, from an automation it generates.

```yaml title="configuration.yaml"
pururu:
  config:
    notify: notify.mobile_app_phone          # where every message goes
  devices:
    clothes_washer:
      name: Tanquinho
      appliance:
        power: sensor.washer_plug_power
        running_program: {above: 4, on_delay: {minutes: 1}, off_delay: {minutes: 2}}
        notifications:
          finished:                           # "The cycle finished."
      reactions:
        door_open:
          name: Porta aberta
          entity: binary_sensor.porta_despensa
          to: "on"
          message: A porta da despensa abriu.

automation pururu: !include_dir_merge_list pururu/automations
```

Your phone gets **Tanquinho** / **The cycle finished.** when a wash ends, and **Tanquinho** / **A porta da despensa abriu.** when the door opens.

## Where a message goes

<Property name="config.notify" type="notify action or list" optional>
  Where every message goes: a `notify` action such as `notify.mobile_app_phone`, or a list of them. A reaction's or a notification's own `notify` replaces it.
</Property>

A message with no `notify` of its own and no `config.notify` is a configuration error: `device clothes_washer: reactions: door_open: message needs notify, here or in config.notify`.

- The title is always the device's name.
- The message is shown **as you wrote it**: a `{` isn't a template.
- Whether the action exists is Home Assistant's to say when it runs: the automation's trace and the log show a `notify` action that doesn't exist.

## A reaction's message

Any [reaction](/concepts/reactions) can tell something when it fires:

<Property name="message" type="string" optional>
  What to tell. It can't be empty.
</Property>

---

<Property name="notify" type="notify action or list" optional>
  Where this message goes, instead of `config.notify`. Only with `message`.
</Property>

With `then`, the program starts first, then the message is told. Every phone is told, even when one of them can't be, such as a phone no longer paired: its `notify` action is gone, and the log says so.

## Ready-made notifications

A feature may offer notifications for what happens to it. Enable one with one key under its block's `notifications`, as you enable its [ready-made alerts](/features/appliance#ready-made-alerts) under `alerts`:

| Notification | Told when | Name | Default message |
|---|---|---|---|
| `appliance: notifications: finished` | a cycle ends (`appliance_running` goes from `on` to `off`) | Finished / Terminou | The cycle finished. / O ciclo terminou. |

Each one takes `message`, which replaces its text, and `notify`, which replaces `config.notify`. `finished:` alone, with nothing after it, is every default.

```yaml
appliance:
  power: sensor.washer_plug_power
  running_program: {above: 4, on_delay: {minutes: 1}, off_delay: {minutes: 2}}
  notifications:
    finished: {message: Roupa pronta!, notify: notify.mobile_app_tablet}
```

- Each one is `automation.pururu_<key>_<feature namespace>_notification_<notification>`, shown as the device's name and its own: `automation.pururu_clothes_washer_appliance_notification_finished`, **Tanquinho Finished** (in your Home Assistant's language).
- A reload tells nobody: `finished` needs `running` to go from `on` straight to `off`.
- If the entity it watches isn't created, it isn't generated either, and the log says why.
- A name the feature doesn't offer is refused: `nope is not a ready-made notification of appliance: finished`. A feature that offers none takes no `notifications`.

## The file and the include

pururu writes the ready-made notifications' automations to `pururu/automations/automations.yaml`, after the reactions': one file for both. One line of `configuration.yaml` includes it:

```yaml title="configuration.yaml"
automation pururu: !include_dir_merge_list pururu/automations
```

Until it's there, **Settings → Repairs** shows **The reactions' and notifications' automations aren't loaded**: one issue for both. See [Reactions: the include](/concepts/reactions#the-include).
