---
title: Dashboard
description: A read-only dashboard at /pururu that lists every floor, area and device pururu manages.
---

pururu adds **Pururu** to Home Assistant's sidebar, at `/pururu`. It's a read-only overview of what pururu manages, and only administrators see it.

It shows:

- **Totals**: how many dashboards (this one), floors, areas and devices pururu manages.
- **A table** of each of them, with its ID, name and type. A floor's or an area's ID is its key under `floors:` or `areas:`. A device's ID is its key under `devices:`.

## Only what pururu manages

Floors, areas and devices made in the UI or by other integrations aren't listed. An existing floor or area appears once pururu has [adopted](/concepts/floors-and-areas#yaml-wins) it.

## Always current

The dashboard isn't saved anywhere. It's built from Home Assistant's registries each time you open it, and an open page refreshes when something it lists changes: a reload, a rename in the UI or a deleted device. Its labels are in Home Assistant's language (English or Portuguese).

It's read-only: the UI offers no **Edit** button. To change what it shows, change the YAML.

## When it doesn't appear

If `/pururu` is already taken by another panel or dashboard, pururu logs `The dashboard is not created: /pururu is already taken` and adds no dashboard. Everything else (floors, areas, devices, entities) is still created. See [Troubleshooting](/reference/troubleshooting#dashboard).
